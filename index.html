Questions

How much does it cost?

The first assessment is free. After it, we recommend a plan based on your player's grade, goals and how much support you need, and give you an exact quote. Every fee is flat and agreed in writing before we start.

Do you guarantee a scholarship or roster spot?

We wish we could, but no one can promise that, and we'd be cautious of anyone who does. Coaches decide based on level, results, academics and roster needs. What we can promise is that your player will be prepared, visible to the right programs, and making informed choices.

When should we start?

Ideally in 9th or 10th grade. Division I coaches can contact players from June 15 after sophomore year, and the work that matters happens before then: playing the right junior tournaments, preparing for the tests your target schools require, and keeping grades and core courses on track. Starting early means your player is ready the day coaches can reach out.

Can you work with us remotely?

Yes. Remote evaluations use match video, UTR, WTN (World Tennis Number) and results. In-person evaluations are available based on your location and service plan.
